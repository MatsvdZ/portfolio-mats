How I work

Process

01

Concept first

I start by figuring out what the project should feel like. A good idea makes the design and code decisions easier.

02

Build quickly

I like getting something on screen fast. It helps me see what works and what is only nice in my head.

03

Test and adjust

I test, ask feedback and change things when the idea does not work as well as expected.

04

Polish the details

Motion, states, responsiveness, accessibility and the small things that make the project feel finished.
